- /deploy — Commit, push, and deploy to Vercel

Tips:
- Be specific in your prompts: "Add a grid of 3 cards with name, image, and description" works better than "Add some cards"
- Give context: paste in your requirements.md or describe your business
- Iterate: "Make the cards bigger" / "Change the color to blue" / "Add a loading spinner"
- If something breaks: press Esc twice to undo, or say "revert the last change"
- Commit often: "Commit this with message 'added contact form'" creates a checkpoint you can go back to
- Check your app: open localhost:3000 in your browser to see changes in real-time
